STANDORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

STANDORA ("the app") is developed by Karam Dallo. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our servers. The app has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Information you enter in the app

Visitor details, company and event information, notes and business card photos that you enter are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party. You control this data and can export or delete it at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Camera

The app asks for camera access only when you choose to take a business card photo. The photo is saved on your device with the visitor record.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share leads (for example as an Excel file), the file goes to the destination you choose. We do not receive it.

Links to other services

Some buttons open other apps or websites, such as maps, WhatsApp or links you add yourself. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for business use and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
