Frequently asked questions

How do I add my company and event?

Open Settings > Company to add your company profile, then Settings > Events to add an event.

How do I add a visitor?

Go to New visitor, fill in the details or take a business card photo, then save. Rate the lead as Hot, Warm or Cold.

How do I export my leads?

Go to Settings > Export and choose the company and event to export.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device. Export your leads regularly to keep a backup, because deleting the app removes its data.

Which languages are supported?

14 languages, including English and Arabic, with light and dark modes.
